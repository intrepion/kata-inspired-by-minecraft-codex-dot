# Glossary

- Glade: the finite land available to explore and modify.
- Block: one cube of a material occupying a cell.
- Cell: one location in the glade.
- Inventory: finite resources carried by the player.
- Board: crafted wooden building material.
- Pick: permanent tool that permits stone gathering.
- Glow: crafted luminous building block.
- Shelter: a player-built resting place; the journey recognizes six boards and a glow.
- Journey: optional opening goals leading into free building.
- Recovery: return to a clear safe starting position without losing resources.
